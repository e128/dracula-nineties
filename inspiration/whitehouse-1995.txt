[LAYOUT]
A centered card floats on white. A gold bordered panel holds a script title, a large photograph
of the White House, six circular seal buttons arranged three per side, and three illustrated
icons below. Nothing is left-aligned; the whole page is one symmetrical composition with no
scroll.

[META]
The page behaves like an invitation or a brochure cover. Ornament carries all the hierarchy:
gold rules, seals, engraved type and a photograph. Every destination is a picture, and the
white margin turns the card into an object rather than a document.

[RELEVANCE]
The centered bordered card and the seal-button ring are a period frame device. The gold rule
and centered script title are worth reusing; the clickable seal ring is not.
