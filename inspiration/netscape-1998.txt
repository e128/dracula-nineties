[LAYOUT]
A black starfield banner holds the logo, over a search band with a select and an input. Below that the page is three columns of link groups under headings, a news item and a select at the lower left, and a right column of two ad panels. A tab row and a link row footer close the page.

[META]
A portal front page in its simplest form. Three columns of links with a search box above and ads at the side, so the page reads as a directory with a shop window. The starfield banner is the only atmosphere, and it is spent entirely on the wordmark.

[RELEVANCE]
The search band with a select and an input is the search strip to reuse, and the three columns of bold link headings each trailing a row of small links are the portal grid. Leave out the starfield banner and the ad column.
