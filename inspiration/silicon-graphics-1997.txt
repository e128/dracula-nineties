[LAYOUT]
A wordmark lockup sits at the top left with a five column link block at the right, set right aligned with rules under each line. The body is a two by two grid of large square images, each with a white caption line, plus a Mars Pathfinder tile at the lower right. Four small badges and a copyright line close the foot.

[META]
A corporate page built from four squares. The images carry all the color and each one gets a single caption line, so the page reads as a contact sheet. The right aligned link block is the only typographic decision, and it is a neat one.

[RELEVANCE]
The caption-line-under-image grid is a clean pattern, and the sample pages already cover it. The colors here are illustrative rather than structural, so nothing transfers.
