Below the masthead the page is a plain hypertext list: a dated news index with red and blue headings, then a second link list, then footer links and a copyright. [META]
The bevel era in one page. Navigation is a vertical stack of 3D pills, content is unadorned Times hypertext, and a single promo box with a starburst is the only place the two languages meet. The masthead is decorated and the document is not, which is the split that later stylesheets exist to remove.

[RELEVANCE]
The beveled button stack is period texture this template does not want. The useful part is the split itself: a decorated header over a plain document body is close to what the sample pages already do.
