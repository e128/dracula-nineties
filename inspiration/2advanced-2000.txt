[LAYOUT]
A full-bleed Flash stage fixed at roughly 760 by 550, centered on a grey field. An eleven-item
uppercase menu bar runs edge to edge across the top. The hero carries a cut-out photographic
figure at left, a grid of thin cyan scanlines, decorative CJK glyphs used as texture, and a
stacked logo block at right. A horizontal ticker band closes the hero, with two status bars
below it, one for section links and one for copyright and an audio toggle.

[META]
Maximalist Flash-era showcase. Chrome and navy, with information density built from layered
translucency, one-pixel rules and very small uppercase type instead of whitespace. Interface
chrome is the ornament, so the menu bar and status bars carry as much visual weight as the
hero. Everything is absolutely positioned inside a stage that assumes a desktop canvas.

[RELEVANCE]
The two-tier bar structure, a nav row plus a separate status row, is the one transferable idea.
The saturated navy palette and the fixed pixel stage are both things this template avoids on
purpose.
