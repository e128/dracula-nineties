[LAYOUT]
A white page with a deep navy title box across the top right, then a narrow left column of icon and label pairs inside a grey border, each row a small illustration over a product name. The center column is a stack of bordered news boxes: a red heading, a short paragraph, then a short blue link list or a photo. A bracketed link row closes the body above the copyright line.

[META]
A corporate home page built from nested boxes rather than from a grid. Each news item is its own bordered panel, so the page reads as a stack of cards, and the icon column acts as a permanent index. The palette is two working colors, navy and dark red, over white.

[RELEVANCE]
The bracketed link row is the nav convention to reuse, as in [About Be] [BeProducts] [What's New], and the icon rail inside a one-pixel grey border is the bordered edge beside it.
