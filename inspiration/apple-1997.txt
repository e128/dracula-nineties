[LAYOUT]
A dark red rail runs the full height at the left, about 130 pixels wide, holding an index of product sections, a region select and a search box. The remaining width is a two-column news feed, each item a bordered box with a red heading, a short paragraph and blue links. Tabs sit at intervals over the feed, and the footer repeats the index as a link row.

[META]
A news-driven corporate home page with a long scroll and a permanent rail. The rail is the strong element, a solid block of dark red that never moves, while the feed to its right is dense text in boxes. The page reads as a newspaper front page with a fixed left index.

[RELEVANCE]
The fixed index rail is a layout this template does not use, and the long two-column feed is a useful reminder of how tiring border-per-item can get. Nothing transfers directly.
