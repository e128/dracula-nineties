[LAYOUT]
A link row of downloads sits above the logo, then a blue search band with a select and a green promo box beside it. The main area is a five-column directory of categories with sub-links, a weather and stock box at the left, and a right column with a world news list over a highlights list. Footer rows and a site list close the page.

[META]
The portal at its most complete. Categories run five across, each a heading over a row of links, so the page reads as a newspaper index and the eye has nowhere to rest. Color appears only in rules and in one promo box.

[RELEVANCE]
The right rail with a dated World News list over Today's Highlights is the dated news rail to reuse, and the pale blue band is the search strip beside it. Leave out the five equal link columns.
