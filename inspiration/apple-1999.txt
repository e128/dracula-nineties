[LAYOUT]
One product photograph takes the top half of the page, with a serif wordmark and two lines of display copy at its left. A tan headline strip sits under a timestamp, then three bordered news tiles run across on a single divider. A black tab strip of eleven links closes the masthead, and below it sit a search row, two link clusters and the copyright.

[META]
The launch page as a poster. One image carries the whole top, the page is otherwise white, and the tan bar is the only band of color. Type does the hierarchy, a serif wordmark against a sans news strip, so the page reads as calm and product-led rather than as a directory.

[RELEVANCE]
The tan headline strip is a useful precedent for a single accent band on an otherwise white page, and the template already has that move in its own palette. Nothing else transfers.
