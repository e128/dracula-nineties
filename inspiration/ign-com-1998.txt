[LAYOUT]
A heavy gold frame with an arched top holds the logo and a tagline. Inside, a gold-bordered left rail of seven red and white buttons sits beside a wide center column of story items and a right column of boxes. Headings are gold bars with white type, and a gold back to top bar closes the page.

[META]
A games portal built as a marquee. The gold frame is the identity, and inside it the page is a stack of headlines with dates, each a thumbnail and a blurb. It reads as a fan magazine cover, and the frame is what stops the black field from reading as empty.

[RELEVANCE]
The gold frame on black is close in spirit to a template ground with one accent, and it is a useful precedent for how far one accent can carry a long page. The layout itself does not transfer.
