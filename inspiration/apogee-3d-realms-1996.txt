[LAYOUT]
A left rail of plain white text links about 100 pixels wide, then a centered column that is mostly headings and short numbered lists. A garland strip of clip-art lights divides the content twice, once below the masthead and once above the modification date. Logos sit at the top corners, and badges, a hit counter and a partner logo stack down the second half of the page.

[META]
A hobbyist-era game page where decoration arrives as clip art rather than as layout. The structure is a heading and a numbered list, repeated, with every spare gap filled by a bitmap. Text is small serif left to run wide, so the page reads as a printed flyer pinned to black.

[RELEVANCE]
The numbered-list rhythm and the single red marker color are usable, but both are already standard here. Nothing else transfers; the clip-art density is the thing this template exists to avoid.
